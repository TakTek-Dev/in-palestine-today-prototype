    In the olive groves, a week after a killing

    06:00Sun

    He irrigated the tree with his blood.

    An olive farmer from the Nablus area, cousin of the man killed on 7 October, to The National

    ‘He irrigated the tree with his blood’: olive farmers brace for a violent harvest

    Nablus — The National visits a Nablus-area grove where a father of four was killed by settlers on 7 October, and a family in Usarin still nursing its wounds.

    In the hills around Nablus, a 59-year-old farmer showed The National the olive tree where his cousin, a 44-year-old father of four, bled to death after settlers attacked the family’s grove on 7 October. He said the attackers fired at them, beat them with metal and wooden objects and threw stones; eight people were hurt, two by gunfire.

    Farther south in the village of Usarin, a 63-year-old woman described being beaten as she ran toward relatives ambushed by settlers in the grove beside their home. One of her sons was still in intensive care in Nablus with a severe brain injury, the paper reported.

    The National says the trees are full this year after a poor 2025 crop, that at least two Palestinians have been killed by settlers since farmers began returning to their groves in late September, and that both attacks it documented took place in Areas A and B, where the Palestinian Authority has full or partial control.

    Amnesty International’s German section has chosen Khaled Quzmar, a Ramallah-based lawyer who has spent nearly four decades defending Palestinian children, for its 2026 Human Rights Award. WAFA reported the award on Wednesday.

    Gaza Strip — Three bodies and 17 injured people reached hospitals in 24 hours; 1,501 people have been killed since the ceasefire began a year ago.

    More than 430 Jerusalem homes and structures demolished this year, researcher says

    East Jerusalem — Fakhri Abu Diab told Voice of Palestine radio that 23,280 demolition orders are in force and that no more than 1% of Jerusalemites who applied this year got permits.

    Israeli authorities have demolished more than 430 homes and other structures in occupied East Jerusalem since the start of 2026, Jerusalem affairs researcher Fakhri Abu Diab told Voice of Palestine radio. He said 23,280 administrative and judicial demolition orders are in force in the city.

    Beit Hanina, al-Tur, Silwan and Jabal al-Mukabbir are among the most targeted neighbourhoods, Abu Diab said. He estimated that about 33 percent of Palestinian homes in Jerusalem are under threat of demolition and put the number demolished since 1967 at about 37,000.

    13:18Sun

    Colombia opens embassy in Jerusalem; Palestinian ministry calls it a violation

    Jerusalem — Colombia is the tenth country with an embassy in the city, Reuters reported; Ramallah says the move breaches Security Council resolutions 476 and 478.

    Colombia opened its embassy to Israel in Jerusalem on Wednesday at a ceremony attended by Israeli Foreign Minister Gideon Sa’ar and Colombian Foreign Minister Omar Bula Escobar, making it the tenth country with an embassy in the city, Reuters reported. Former president Gustavo Petro broke off relations with Israel in May 2024; his successor, Abelardo de la Espriella, restored them, and a free trade agreement was reinstated on Tuesday.

    The Palestinian Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Expatriates condemned the opening as a violation of international law and of Security Council resolutions 476 and 478, urged Bogotá to reverse it and called on states to take political, diplomatic, economic and legal steps against any country that opens or moves an embassy to Jerusalem.

    Palestine’s envoy to the Arab League, Muhannad al-Aklouk, told Voice of Palestine radio that Arab states had decided to summon Colombia’s ambassadors and had asked members of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation to do the same.
