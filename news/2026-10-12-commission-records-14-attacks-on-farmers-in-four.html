Commission records 14 attacks on farmers in four governorates in 12 hours

Occupied West Bank — Eight were in Ramallah and al-Bireh; in Yasuf, settlers stole olives and a harvesting machine.

Figures · Monday 12 October 2026, filed 19:18 Palestine time · Source: WAFA / Colonization and Wall Resistance Commission

Edited by Yousef Khalil, West Bank desk

The Colonization and Wall Resistance Commission recorded 14 attacks on farmers, their land and crops between 6 a.m. and 6 p.m. on Monday in the Ramallah and al-Bireh, Salfit, Nablus and Hebron governorates: ten by settlers, three by the army and one carried out jointly, it said.

Eight were in Ramallah and al-Bireh, including in Turmus Ayya, Burqa, Kafr Malik and Deir Dibwan, where a farmer’s car was smashed, the Commission said. In Yasuf, east of Salfit, settlers drove farmers off their land and stole olives and a harvesting machine, village council head Jum’a Abdul Fattah told WAFA; settlers also pitched a tent near the Iskaka spring.

In Mukhmas, north of Jerusalem, two settlers chased a shepherd and tried to steal his sheep before raiding olive groves and taking the fruit, the Jerusalem Governorate said.
