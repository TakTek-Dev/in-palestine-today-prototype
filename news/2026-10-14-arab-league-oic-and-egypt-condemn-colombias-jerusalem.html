Arab League, OIC and Egypt condemn Colombia’s Jerusalem embassy

Cairo — The League cited Security Council resolutions 478 and 2334; the OIC chief wrote to Colombia’s foreign minister urging a reversal and warned of consequences.

Statement · Wednesday 14 October 2026, filed 18:15 Palestine time · Source: WAFA (Arab League, OIC, Egyptian Foreign Ministry statements)

The Arab League condemned Colombia’s opening of an embassy in Jerusalem as contrary to international resolutions, citing Security Council Resolution 478 of 1980, which called on states to withdraw diplomatic missions from the city, and Resolution 2334 of 2016. It said the step came despite repeated warnings and contacts with Bogotá and urged Colombia to reverse it.

Organisation of Islamic Cooperation Secretary-General Ismail Ould Cheikh Ahmed said the move violates international law and Resolution 478, wrote to Colombia’s foreign minister urging him to reverse it and warned of far-reaching consequences for Colombia’s relations with the Muslim world, WAFA reported.

Egypt’s Foreign Ministry also rejected the step, calling unilateral measures to change Jerusalem’s legal and historical status null and void.
