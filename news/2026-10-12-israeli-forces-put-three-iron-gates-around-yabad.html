Israeli forces put three iron gates around Ya’bad and start a military road into Jenin camp

Jenin — Ya’bad municipality said the gates endanger lives by delaying ambulances; in Jenin, bulldozers began a road on land seized under a new military order.

Update · Monday 12 October 2026, filed 10:31 Palestine time · Source: WAFA / Ya'bad municipality; Colonization and Wall Resistance Commission

Edited by Yousef Khalil, West Bank desk

Israeli forces installed three iron gates on Monday at the entrances to Ya’bad, southwest of Jenin, and on the roads to Kufeirit and Kafr Qud, Ya’bad municipality told WAFA. It said another gate on the town’s western side had been closed for more than 20 days.

The municipality said the gates delay residents on their way to work, school and clinics, hold up ambulances, and further isolate the town and surrounding villages. Israeli forces had earlier installed more than ten gates at village entrances east of Jenin, WAFA said.

In the afternoon Israeli bulldozers began cutting a new road linking the al-Jabriyat military post to Jenin refugee camp, after taking 741 square metres of Palestinian land for ‘military purposes’, WAFA reported. The Colonization and Wall Resistance Commission said the land was seized under military order 33/26, dated 5 October, in an area of Jenin classified as Area A, and that the order allows 24 hours for objections after a site visit with the landowners.
