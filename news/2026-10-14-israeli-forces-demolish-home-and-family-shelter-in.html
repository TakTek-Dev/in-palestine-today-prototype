Israeli forces demolish home and family shelter in Masafer Yatta, sources say

Masafer Yatta, south of Hebron — An 80-square-metre house in Ighzaywa and a 40-square-metre shelter for a family of seven in Umm al-Khair were razed; a mobile toilet was seized.

Update · Wednesday 14 October 2026, filed 10:50 Palestine time · Source: WAFA

Israeli forces demolished an 80-square-metre house in Khirbet Ighzaywa, south of Yatta, and a 40-square-metre dwelling that housed a family of seven in Khirbet Umm al-Khair, in the Masafer Yatta area south of Hebron, local sources told WAFA. They also seized a mobile toilet.

UN OCHA reported on 2 October that about 400 structures had been demolished in the Masafer Yatta communities located inside the area Israel designates Firing Zone 918, displacing 969 Palestinians.
