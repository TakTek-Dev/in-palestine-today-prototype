Palestinian officials win court order to remove settler fence in northern Jordan Valley

al-Farisiya, northern Jordan Valley — Settlers had fenced land of the Nabe’ Ghazal community in al-Farisiya; Israeli forces took the fence down after the ruling, a Tubas official said.

Update · Tuesday 13 October 2026, filed 23:04 Palestine time · Source: WAFA / Tubas Governorate

Edited by Yousef Khalil, West Bank desk

The Colonization and Wall Resistance Commission and Tubas Governorate obtained a court ruling on Tuesday ordering the removal of a fence that settlers had put up on land belonging to the Nabe’ Ghazal community in al-Farisiya, in the northern Jordan Valley, WAFA reported.

Mutaz Basharat, who handles settlement affairs for Tubas Governorate, said Israeli forces removed the fence after the ruling. The report did not name the court.
