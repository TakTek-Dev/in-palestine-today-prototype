WAFA’s daily round-up: one killed in Gaza; Commission counts 25 attacks on farmers in seven governorates

West Bank and Gaza — The closing tallies cover the Ramallah raid, settler attacks from Talfit to Masafer Yatta, and demolitions in Jerusalem and Jenin.

Report · Tuesday 13 October 2026, filed 23:53 Palestine time · Source: WAFA (daily round-up); Colonization and Wall Resistance Commission

WAFA’s end-of-day round-up said one Palestinian was killed in the strike on a car in Gaza City’s al-Tuffah on Tuesday and listed the injuries in the Ramallah raid, the arrests around Nablus, Hebron and Bethlehem, and an incursion into Tuqu’, southeast of Bethlehem.

It also recorded the demolition of an uninhabited house in al-Jalama, north of Jenin, of greenhouses near the bypass roundabout, and the installation of an iron gate at the main entrance to Arraba, south of Jenin.

The Colonization and Wall Resistance Commission said it recorded 25 attacks on farmers and land by settlers and Israeli forces between 6 a.m. and 6 p.m. in seven governorates: six each in Nablus and Hebron, five in Ramallah and al-Bireh, three each in Salfit and Tulkarm, and one each in Jerusalem and Jenin. They included attacks in Yatma, Duma and Aqraba near Nablus, olive theft by settlers from Susya in Yatta, and farmers barred from their land in Beit Anan, northwest of Jerusalem.
