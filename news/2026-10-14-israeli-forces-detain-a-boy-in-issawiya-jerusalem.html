Israeli forces detain a boy in Issawiya, Jerusalem Governorate says

Issawiya, East Jerusalem — Forces and Israeli municipal crews also entered the al-Bustan neighbourhood of Silwan, south of al-Aqsa Mosque, the governorate said.

Update · Wednesday 14 October 2026, filed 10:23 Palestine time · Source: WAFA / Jerusalem Governorate

Edited by Dina Nasser, Jerusalem desk

Israeli forces detained a boy after raiding his family’s home in Issawiya, in occupied East Jerusalem, before dawn on Wednesday, the Jerusalem Governorate said. His age was not given.

Israeli forces and crews from the Israeli-run Jerusalem municipality also entered the al-Bustan neighbourhood of Silwan, south of al-Aqsa Mosque, the governorate said.
