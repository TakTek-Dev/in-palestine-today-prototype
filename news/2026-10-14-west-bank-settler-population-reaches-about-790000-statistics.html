West Bank settler population reaches about 790,000, statistics bureau says

West Bank — A statement for Arab Environment Day says outposts set up in 2023-2025 control about 1.07 million dunums and displaced 118 herding communities.

Figures · Wednesday 14 October 2026, filed 09:28 Palestine time · Source: WAFA / PCBS and Environment Quality Authority

About 790,000 settlers lived in the occupied West Bank at the end of July 2026, spread across 170 settlements and 400 outposts, the Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics and the Environment Quality Authority said in a joint statement for Arab Environment Day.

Rangeland covers about 2,111 square kilometres, roughly 37 percent of the West Bank, the statement said. It said 185 new outposts set up between 2023 and 2025, about 130 of them herding outposts and hilltops, control around 1.07 million dunums, and that settler violence and blocked access to pasture and water displaced 118 herding communities over the same period.

Separately, Amir Daoud of the Colonization and Wall Resistance Commission told Voice of Palestine radio that more than 110 Bedouin communities had been partly or fully displaced in the past three years.
