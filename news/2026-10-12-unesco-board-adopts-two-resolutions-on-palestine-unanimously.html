UNESCO board adopts two resolutions on Palestine unanimously

UNESCO headquarters, Paris — They cover cultural heritage in Jerusalem, Hebron and Bethlehem and educational institutions in the occupied territories.

Statement · Monday 12 October 2026, filed 19:33 Palestine time · Source: WAFA

UNESCO’s Executive Board, at its 225th session, unanimously adopted two resolutions on Palestine, one on the occupied Palestinian territory and one on educational and cultural institutions in the occupied Arab territories, WAFA reported. They cover the protection of heritage sites in Jerusalem, Hebron and Bethlehem and of schools and cultural institutions.

Palestine’s ambassador to UNESCO, Adel Atieh, said attacks on schools, universities, heritage sites and journalists strike at the core of the organisation’s mandate. Palestinian National Council President Rouhi Fattouh welcomed the vote and called for the resolutions to be turned into binding measures, WAFA said.
