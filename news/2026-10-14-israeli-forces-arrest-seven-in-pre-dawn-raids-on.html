Israeli forces arrest seven in pre-dawn raids on Nablus and Qalqilya, WAFA says

Nablus — Troops also searched homes and questioned residents in Deir Dibwan, Burqa, Turmus Ayya and Qibya in Ramallah and al-Bireh governorate, security sources said.

Update · Wednesday 14 October 2026, filed 08:33 Palestine time · Source: WAFA

Edited by Yousef Khalil, West Bank desk

Israeli forces arrested four Palestinians in Nablus before dawn on Wednesday after raiding the al-Ma’ajin and al-Dahiya neighbourhoods and the Askar and al-Ein refugee camps and searching homes, security and local sources told WAFA.

In Qalqilya, troops entered from the city’s southern entrance, raided homes on al-Wad Street and arrested three men, among them a former prisoner, local sources said. Quds News Network put the number arrested in Qalqilya at five.

In Ramallah and al-Bireh governorate, forces raided Deir Dibwan and Burqa to the east, Turmus Ayya to the north and Qibya to the west, searching homes and questioning their owners on the spot, security sources said (WAFA, 08:43).
