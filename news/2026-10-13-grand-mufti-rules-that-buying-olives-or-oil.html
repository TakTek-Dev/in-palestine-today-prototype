Grand Mufti rules that buying olives or oil stolen by settlers is forbidden

Jerusalem — Sheikh Mohammad Hussein said the purchase would empower those taking Palestinian land.

Statement · Tuesday 13 October 2026, filed 13:49 Palestine time · Source: WAFA / Grand Mufti of Jerusalem and Palestine

Edited by Dina Nasser, Jerusalem desk

The Grand Mufti of Jerusalem and Palestine, Sheikh Mohammad Hussein, issued a fatwa on Tuesday forbidding Palestinians to buy olives or olive oil taken from Palestinian trees and sold by settlers, WAFA reported.

He said buying stolen goods is forbidden because the seller does not own them, and that such purchases encourage the seizure of Palestinian land and the displacement of its owners. He called on official bodies and the public to refuse to buy or sell such produce.
