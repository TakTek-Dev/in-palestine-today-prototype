PLO refugee official warns UNRWA against laying off staff over budget gap

Ramallah — Ahmad Abu Holi wrote to Commissioner-General Christian Saunders after Saunders said in Berlin that large layoffs were possible without new funding.

Statement · Wednesday 14 October 2026, filed 16:12 Palestine time · Source: WAFA / PLO Department of Refugee Affairs

Ahmad Abu Holi, a PLO Executive Committee member who heads its Department of Refugee Affairs, warned UNRWA against laying off or cutting staff because of the deficit in its regular budget, saying it would threaten the agency’s stability and its ability to serve Palestinian refugees.

In a letter to UNRWA Commissioner-General Christian Saunders, Abu Holi voiced concern at remarks Saunders made at the Diplomatic Academy in Berlin about possible large-scale layoffs if additional funding does not arrive soon. He rejected a smaller agency built on staff cuts and proposed temporary alternatives, including deferring salary payments where necessary, until funding is secured.

Closing the funding gap is the international community’s responsibility and must not come at the expense of staff or refugees, Abu Holi said.
