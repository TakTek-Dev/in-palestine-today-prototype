Israel Land Authority says E1 settlement bids will wait until after the election

Occupied East Jerusalem — Tenders for 1,234 housing units will not be reviewed before 19 November, after the attorney general ordered a delay.

Update · Monday 12 October 2026, filed 11:16 Palestine time · Source: The Times of Israel / Israel Land Authority

Edited by Dina Nasser, Jerusalem desk

The Israel Land Authority said tenders for 1,234 housing units in the E1 settlement project, east of occupied East Jerusalem, will not be reviewed before 19 November, The Times of Israel reported. The delay follows an order by Israel’s attorney general over concern that the process could be used for campaigning before the 27 October election, the paper said.

E1 lies between the Ma’ale Adumim settlement and Jerusalem.
