US State Department claims ‘real progress’ toward a demilitarised Gaza a year after Sharm el-Sheikh

Washington — It said rebuilding will be ‘an arduous, multi-year process’; the claims were not independently verified.

Statement · Tuesday 13 October 2026, filed 18:00 Palestine time · Source: The Times of Israel / US State Department

The US State Department said on the anniversary of the Sharm el-Sheikh summit that ‘real progress’ is being made toward a demilitarised Gaza, The Times of Israel reported. It credited the Trump administration’s plan with producing a lasting ceasefire, the return of all hostages, the creation of the Board of Peace and a Palestinian technocratic administration, and a fully costed recovery plan, and said Hamas theft of aid had ‘dwindled’.

‘Rebuilding Gaza will be an arduous, multi-year process,’ the statement said. The paper noted that the claims were the department’s own. Gaza health officials have counted 1,498 people killed since the ceasefire, according to medical sources quoted by WAFA the same day.
