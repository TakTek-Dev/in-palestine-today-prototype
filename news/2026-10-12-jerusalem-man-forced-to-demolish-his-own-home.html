Jerusalem man forced to demolish his own home; two more homes in Silwan face demolition

Occupied East Jerusalem — The Beit Hanina home belonged to a man married a year ago and expecting his first child, the Jerusalem Governorate said.

Update · Monday 12 October 2026, filed 19:11 Palestine time · Source: WAFA / Jerusalem Governorate

Edited by Dina Nasser, Jerusalem desk

Israel’s Jerusalem municipality forced a Palestinian to demolish his own 60-square-metre home in the al-Ashqariya neighbourhood of Beit Hanina on Monday, the Jerusalem Governorate said. The house was built in 2017; its owner married about a year ago and is expecting his first child, the governorate said.

Israeli authorities also served demolition orders on the homes of a father and son in the Wadi Qaddum neighbourhood of Silwan, south of Al-Aqsa, the governorate said. The son had begun emptying both homes to demolish them himself; his 100-square-metre house, built in 2002, is home to his wife and four children, and his parents’ house measures 60 square metres.

WAFA said Israel rarely grants Palestinians building permits in Jerusalem, and that those who refuse to demolish their homes themselves face demolition by municipal bulldozers and heavy costs.
