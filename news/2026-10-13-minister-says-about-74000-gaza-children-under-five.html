Minister says about 74,000 Gaza children under five are malnourished

Gaza Strip — Social Development Minister Samah Hamad also told an Arab League meeting that the number displaced in the West Bank is nearing 50,000.

Statement · Tuesday 13 October 2026, filed 20:00 Palestine time · Source: WAFA

Edited by Hala Abu Sitta, Gaza desk

  • X

About 74,000 children under the age of five in Gaza are suffering from malnutrition, Palestinian Social Development Minister Samah Hamad said in a recorded address to the executive bureau of the Arab Council of Social Affairs Ministers, meeting at Arab League headquarters in Cairo on Tuesday.

Hamad said families have lived in tents for three years and that the food aid entering Gaza does not meet their needs. In the West Bank, she said, the number of people displaced by Israeli raids and attacks is approaching 50,000.

She said the government keeps providing basic services despite a financial crisis and the withholding of about $6.5 billion in Palestinian funds, and that the ministry’s social registry covers about 350,000 families. The bureau approved a ministry programme of cash aid for displaced people with disabilities in Tulkarm and Jenin, to be put to the ministerial council, Palestine’s delegate said.
