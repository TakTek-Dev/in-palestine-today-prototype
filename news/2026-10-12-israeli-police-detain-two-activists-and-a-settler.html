Israeli police detain two activists and a settler teen after a clash at a Palestinian olive harvest

Near Duma, south of Nablus — A left-wing group says its volunteers pushed away a settler who tried to attack two Palestinian women; a right-wing site says the activists beat a settler.

Update · Monday 12 October 2026, filed 18:38 Palestine time · Source: The Times of Israel

Israeli police detained two Israeli activists, one of them a reserve brigadier general and former fighter pilot, on suspicion of assaulting a settler shepherd near the Michtam LeDavid farm, close to the Palestinian village of Duma, south of Nablus, The Times of Israel reported. The retired officer was released shortly after questioning, the paper said.

The group Looking the Occupation in the Eye said its activists, joined by retired senior officers, had come to help Palestinians pick olives, and that they pushed away a settler who tried to attack two Palestinian women carrying sacks of olives; it said police and soldiers took statements only from settlers. The right-wing site Hakol Hayehudi said three activists kicked, punched and beat a settler with a stick.

Police later said a 15-year-old suspected of assault had also been detained, questioned and released, according to The Times of Israel. Police said soldiers had dispersed those involved before officers arrived.
