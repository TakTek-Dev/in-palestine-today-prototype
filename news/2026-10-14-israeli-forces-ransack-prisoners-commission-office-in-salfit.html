Israeli forces ransack prisoners’ commission office in Salfit, demolish Haris workshops

Salfit and Haris, Salfit governorate — Troops tore photographs and questioned staff at the office, its director said; three vehicle-repair shops owned by two brothers were razed in Haris.

Update · Wednesday 14 October 2026, filed 13:52 Palestine time · Source: WAFA

Israeli forces raided the Salfit office of the Commission of Detainees’ and Ex-Detainees’ Affairs on Wednesday, scattering its contents, tearing photographs inside and questioning staff on the spot, the office director told WAFA. Troops also took up positions on a rooftop in the city and blocked traffic, local sources said.

In Haris, west of Salfit, Israeli forces demolished three vehicle-repair and bodywork shops near the village traffic lights belonging to two brothers, measuring about 120, 170 and 150 square metres, local sources said. The report gave no reason for the demolition.
