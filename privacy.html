Privacy

What this site keeps about you, and what it does not.

Sample text for the outlet’s lawyer to review before launch.

12:25Noon

What we keep

If you sign up for The Day by email, we keep your email address to send it, and nothing else. You can leave at any time from the link at the foot of every email, or by writing to newsletter@inpalestine.today.

What stays on your device

The site remembers three things in your own browser, never on our servers: the light you chose (Palestine’s light, always light or always dark), the last part of the day you read, and that you have already seen the opening animation this session. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

What we do not do

There are no advertising trackers, no third-party analytics and no cookies on this site. Share buttons are plain links: nothing is sent to a network until you choose to share.

The host

The site is served by its web host, which keeps standard server logs (address, time and page requested) for security, as every web host does.

Questions

Write to privacy@inpalestine.today. In Palestine Today Media, PO Box 0000, Ramallah, Palestine.
