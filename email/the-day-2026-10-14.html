Settlers set fire to a mosque in Mukhmas after midnight, governorate says

They also burned a car and an olive tree, and AFP footage showed Hebrew graffiti on its walls. It is one of 23 arson attacks or attempts on West Bank mosques counted since January 2023 by the Colonization and Wall Resistance Commission.
