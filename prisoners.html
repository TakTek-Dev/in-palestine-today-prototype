    Amnesty Germany gives 2026 human rights award to children’s lawyer Khaled Quzmar

    The Ramallah-based lawyer has defended Palestinian children for nearly 40 years; the prize will be presented in Berlin on 25 November, WAFA reported.

    Story · Prisoners · Amnesty International Germany / WAFA

  2. 12:36

    Two women held in Damon prison have scabies, prisoners’ commission says

    The detainees, aged 20 and 31, told a commission lawyer their rooms were raided with gas and sound grenades on 7 October and that food is scarce.

    Update · Prisoners · WAFA / Commission of Detainees' and Ex-Detainees' Affairs

    More than 2,000 children detained since October 2023, Prisoners’ Society says

    At least 350 remain in custody, the society said, after at least six children were detained overnight in Sa’ir and al-Shuyukh, near Hebron.

    Figures · Prisoners · Quds News Network / Palestinian Prisoners' Society
