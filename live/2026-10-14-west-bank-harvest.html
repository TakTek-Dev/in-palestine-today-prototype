  1. 19:34Sun

    Commission records 21 attacks on olive harvest across seven governorates in one day

    West Bank — Settlers carried out 12, the army 8 and one was joint, between 6 a.m. and 6 p.m., the Colonization and Wall Resistance Commission said.

    Israeli forces and settlers carried out 21 attacks on farmers, their land and the olive crop in seven governorates between 6 a.m. and 6 p.m. on Wednesday, the Colonization and Wall Resistance Commission said in an evening report: 12 by settlers, 8 by the army and one joint attack.

  2. 19:13Sun

    Settlers attack Qawawis homes in Masafer Yatta; elderly man hurt, owner arrested

    Masafer Yatta, south of Hebron — Settlers smashed doors and cameras before Israeli forces arrived and detained the owner of the damaged house; olives were stolen east of Yatta, an activist said.

    Settlers attacked homes in Khirbet Qawawis in Masafer Yatta, south of Hebron, on Wednesday, bruising an elderly man who was taken by ambulance to Yatta’s government hospital, a local activist told WAFA.

    Update · Source: WAFA · The full report

  3. 19:00Sun

    Settlers beat three Palestinians in Abu Nujeim, southeast of Bethlehem, WAFA reports

    Abu Nujeim, southeast of Bethlehem — One of the men was taken to hospital; the area has faced repeated attacks, including gunfire at residents’ cars in August, WAFA said.

    A group of settlers stormed the Abu Nujeim area southeast of Bethlehem on Wednesday and beat three Palestinians, one of whom was taken to hospital for treatment, WAFA’s correspondent reported.

  4. 18:41Sun

    Ministry condemns Mukhmas mosque arson as commission counts 23 since 2023

    Mukhmas, northeast of Jerusalem — Racist slogans were sprayed on the Sultan Ibrahim Mosque, the ministry said; the commission counts 13 arson attacks on mosques so far in 2026.

    The Palestinian Ministry of Foreign Affairs condemned the burning of the Sultan Ibrahim Mosque in Mukhmas, northeast of Jerusalem, before dawn on Wednesday, saying settlers also burned residents’ property and olive trees and sprayed racist and threatening slogans on the mosque walls.

  5. 17:25Sun

    Israel’s army chief warns of ‘potential for escalation,’ condemns settler violence

    Occupied West Bank — Lt. Gen. Eyal Zamir said the army would keep enabling the olive harvest; the 98th Division has been sent to the West Bank, The Times of Israel reported.

    Israeli military chief of staff Lt. Gen. Eyal Zamir said numerous alerts on all fronts had increased the potential for escalation, during a visit to Central Command headquarters with its head, Maj. Gen. Avi Bluth, The Times of Israel reported.

    Harming and committing violence against innocent people does not reflect the Israeli spirit or the Jewish spirit.

    Eyal Zamir, Chief of Staff, Israeli military

    Statement · Source: The Times of Israel · The full report

  6. 12:14Sun

    Israeli bulldozers uproot hundreds of olive trees in Kafr Malik, sources say

    Kafr Malik, east of Ramallah — Thirty-five dunums of farmland beside the bypass road were razed under a military order issued ten days earlier, local sources told WAFA.

    Israeli bulldozers began razing 35 dunums of farmland in the southern part of Kafr Malik, east of Ramallah, early on Wednesday, uprooting hundreds of fruit-bearing olive trees along the bypass road, local sources told WAFA.

  7. 11:31Sun

    Settlers beat 17-year-old picking olives in Beita, Red Crescent says

    Beita, south of Nablus — In nearby Beit Furik, settlers stopped farmers harvesting east of al-Naqra road, in Area B, and dumped the olives they had gathered, local sources said.

    A 17-year-old was injured when settlers beat him while he and other farmers were picking olives in Beita, south of Nablus, the Palestine Red Crescent Society said. Local sources told WAFA that settlers attacked the pickers and beat them.

  8. 11:27Sun

    Settlers try to stop olive harvest in Turmus Ayya; residents push them back, sources say

    Turmus Ayya, north of Ramallah — The settlers retreated toward an outpost set up on the town’s plain late last year, local sources told WAFA.

    A group of settlers attacked olive pickers in the northeast of Turmus Ayya, north of Ramallah, on Wednesday morning and tried to stop the harvest, local sources told WAFA. Residents confronted them and forced them back toward the outpost settlers set up on the town’s plain.

  9. 11:00Sun

    Settlers bulldoze land in Beit Dajan and Burin to prepare new outpost, sources say

    Burin, south of Nablus — Two bulldozers opened new roads east of Beit Dajan and razed land around a family’s home in Burin, local and security sources told WAFA.

    Settlers accompanied by two bulldozers razed Palestinian land in Beit Dajan, east of Nablus, and Burin, to the south, on Wednesday morning, local and security sources told WAFA.

    Update · Source: WAFA · The full report

  10. 10:08Sun

    Troops tear-gas Jenin-area olive pickers despite prior coordination, sources say

    al-Fandaqumiya, south of Jenin — Farmers from al-Fandaqumiya, Jaba’ and Silat al-Dhahr were forced out; in Ya’bad, settlers stole harvested olives and expelled pickers, local sources said.

    Israeli forces fired tear gas at farmers from al-Fandaqumiya, Jaba’ and Silat al-Dhahr, south of Jenin, as they headed to their groves on Wednesday and forced them to leave, even though access had been coordinated in advance, local sources told WAFA.

    Update · Source: WAFA · The full report

  11. 09:28Sun

    West Bank settler population reaches about 790,000, statistics bureau says

    West Bank — A statement for Arab Environment Day says outposts set up in 2023-2025 control about 1.07 million dunums and displaced 118 herding communities.

    About 790,000 settlers lived in the occupied West Bank at the end of July 2026, spread across 170 settlements and 400 outposts, the Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics and the Environment Quality Authority said in a joint statement for Arab Environment Day.

  12. 09:00Sun

    Settlers set fire to mosque, car and olive tree in Mukhmas, governorate says

    Mukhmas, northeast of Jerusalem — The attack came after midnight in the village northeast of Jerusalem; the Israeli military said no suspects were identified and it had opened an investigation.

    Israeli settlers entered Mukhmas, northeast of Jerusalem, after midnight and set fire to a mosque, an olive tree beside it and a car, which burned out completely, the Jerusalem Governorate said.

    a barbaric act and a dangerous escalation in settler terrorism

    Rouhi Fattouh, President, Palestinian National Council

    Update · Source: WAFA / Jerusalem Governorate; AFP · The full report

  13. 06:00Sun

    ‘He irrigated the tree with his blood’: olive farmers brace for a violent harvest

    Nablus — The National visits a Nablus-area grove where a father of four was killed by settlers on 7 October, and a family in Usarin still nursing its wounds.

    In the hills around Nablus, a 59-year-old farmer showed The National the olive tree where his cousin, a 44-year-old father of four, bled to death after settlers attacked the family’s grove on 7 October. He said the attackers fired at them, beat them with metal and wooden objects and threw stones; eight people were hurt, two by gunfire.

    He irrigated the tree with his blood.

    An olive farmer from the Nablus area, cousin of the man killed on 7 October, to The National
